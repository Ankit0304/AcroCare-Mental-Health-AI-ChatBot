No. ACROCARE is an AI-driven support companion and wellness tool designed to foster self-awareness and emotional regulation. It does not provide clinical diagnosis or medical treatment.

Yes, absolutely. Your chat history and journal entries are securely associated with your authenticated account and are never shared publicly.
